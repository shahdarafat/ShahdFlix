Frequently Asked Questions

ShahdFlix is a streaming-style website concept where you can explore movies, TV shows, anime, documentaries, and more from your connected devices.

This student project is a front-end demonstration. No real subscription or payment is collected.

The responsive layout is designed for phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop screens.

There is no paid membership in this demo, so there is nothing to cancel.

The reference design highlights films, TV shows, anime, documentaries, and original entertainment.

The design includes a separate kids-profile feature to represent a family-friendly viewing space.
